# Owner guide

## Company

Edit `company.yaml` to set your company identity, reply language, timezone and approved signature. `reply.editing.mode: literal` keeps approved blocks unchanged; `mixed` permits constrained rewriting of explicitly editable facts and questions. Prices, conditions and signature remain literal.

## Knowledge

Edit `knowledge.yaml` to define topics with stable IDs, recognition terms and descriptions. Add approved facts and clarification questions referencing those topics. `reply.matching: keywords` uses recognition terms; `ai` allows semantic classification. Uncertain or unsupported requests go to staff.

## Pricing

Edit `pricing.yaml`. Use `prices: {}` until prices are approved. Fixed prices require a quoted amount, currency, complete conditions and quoted validity dates. The app does not calculate bespoke totals or taxes.

## Saving and restoration

Save all three files together. Invalid packages and stale versions cannot replace the active configuration. Copy your changes before reloading after a conflict. Restoration creates a new version from the immediately previous package. Existing drafts are never regenerated by configuration changes.
